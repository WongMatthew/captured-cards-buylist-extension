Captured! Cards — Buylist Graphics (Chrome extension)

INSTALL (about 1 minute)
1. Unzip this file. You'll get a folder called "captured-cards-buylist".
2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the "captured-cards-buylist" folder.
5. Pin it: click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to
   "Captured! Cards Buylist Graphics".

USE
Click the toolbar icon. The maker opens in its own tab.
1. Add cards (or drop images on the big box to create cards from files).
2. Fill in post details (date, rates, call to action, colours).
3. Tick the templates you want.
4. Download a single PNG, or "Download all (.zip)".

5. Post with Muse: choose where (Instagram feed/story, Facebook post/story),
   the caption, and when (post now after approval, or a peak time inside a
   window), then "Review and send to Muse".

POSTING WITH MUSE
Muse has no public API that other apps can send tasks to, and it publishes
through a social posting connector you add inside Muse. So the extension:
  - saves the images to Downloads/CapturedCards/buylist-<date-time>/
  - writes Muse's instructions (also saved there as muse-message.txt)
  - copies the instructions and opens Muse when you click the button.
In Muse: paste, attach the images, send. Muse shows a staging preview and
posts or schedules only after you reply "approve".
One-time setup is under "Muse setup" in step 5.

Your card list, settings and images are saved inside Chrome on this computer.
Everything runs offline; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

UPDATING
Replace the folder's contents with the new version, then click the reload
arrow on the extension's card in chrome://extensions. Your saved list stays.

Keep the folder where it is: Chrome loads the extension from it.
Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers.
