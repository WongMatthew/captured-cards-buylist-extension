Muse setup (one time)

Muse can see your Instagram and Facebook, but it publishes and schedules through a social posting connector. Set this up once in Muse:

  1. In Muse, open Connectors and add a social posting connector, for example Postiz, Buffer or PostFast. Connect your Instagram account and your Facebook Page through it.
  2. Make sure the Instagram account is a Business or Creator account and is linked to the Facebook Page. Instagram only allows posting by apps to those account types.
  3. Save the instructions below as a Muse skill, so every buylist post goes through the same staging step.
