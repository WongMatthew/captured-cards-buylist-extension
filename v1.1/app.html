Connect Shopify (this computer only)

Lets this computer publish the "We're buying" cards to the website and upload their photos. The Client ID and secret stay in this browser; they aren't in the extension files. Only set this up on computers you trust.

  1. Open the Shopify Dev Dashboard and choose the app you use for the website.
  2. Open Settings and copy the Client ID and Client secret into the boxes below.
  3. The app needs the write_metaobjects and write_files permissions (yours already has them).

You can also edit the cards in Shopify admin: Content › Metaobjects › We're buying card.
