Captured! Cards - Buylist graphics (Chrome extension)

Install (once per computer):
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it (e.g. Documents). Don't delete it afterwards.
2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose this folder.
5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin "Captured! Cards - Buylist graphics".

Connect Shopify (once per computer, only on computers you trust):
- Click the gear button next to "Reload from website".
- Paste the Client ID and Client secret from the Shopify Dev Dashboard (your website app > Settings), then Connect.

Fill in cards from photos (optional, once per computer):
- Click the gear button and paste an Anthropic API key (console.anthropic.com > API keys), then Save.
- Now dropping card photos fills in the name, number, set, rarity and slab grade. Fields you've typed are kept.
- The sparkle button on a card re-reads its photo. Each read costs about a cent. Always check the details and add your price.

Use:
- Opening the tool loads the website's "We're buying" cards.
- Edit, add (drop photos in), reorder or delete cards. The first card is the top buy.
- Click "Publish to website": the website's "We're buying" section updates within seconds.
- Pick templates and click "Download all (.zip)" for the social/web graphics.

Updating the extension: replace this folder's files with the new version, then click the refresh arrow on the
extension in chrome://extensions. Your connection and cards stay.
